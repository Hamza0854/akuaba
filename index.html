About us

Compassionate, personalised and advanced healthcare

Akuaba Medical Centre is a multi-specialty healthcare facility in Kumasi. It began as Rayben Diagnostic Centre, focused on diagnostics and laboratory work. As patients asked for more than tests, the facility grew into a full medical centre with outpatient clinics, wards, a theatre and an emergency service.

Our mission is to deliver affordable, accessible and specialised healthcare tailored to individuals, families and corporate clients — with trained professionals and modern equipment behind every diagnosis.

Why choose us

You’re special — and we show it every day

Thoughtful follow-ups, careful medication management and a calm place to recover. The details are what make the difference between treatment and care.

  1. Personalised follow-ups

    We call patients after discharge to check on recovery, make sure treatment plans are being followed, and catch problems early.

  2. Safe, timely medication management

    Our team administers medication accurately and explains proper usage and possible side effects before you go home.

  3. A comfortable healing environment

    Clean, well-organised and calm spaces, because recovery depends on more than the prescription.
